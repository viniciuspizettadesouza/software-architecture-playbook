# SOLID

## What it means

SOLID groups five object-oriented design principles: Single Responsibility, Open/Closed, Liskov Substitution, Interface Segregation and Dependency Inversion.

## Why it matters

Together they encourage focused responsibilities, substitutable contracts and controlled dependency direction. Their value is better changeability, not compliance for its own sake.

## When useful

Use the principles as questions during design and review, especially around modules with several responsibilities, volatile integrations or growing interfaces.

## Don't overdo it

SOLID does not require a class or interface for every operation. Applying it mechanically can create indirection, fragmentation and speculative extension points.
