# Twelve-Factor App

## What it means

Twelve-Factor describes practices for services that are portable across environments: explicit dependencies, environment configuration, attached resources, stateless processes, disposability, log streams and development/production parity.

## Why it matters

The practices reduce environment-specific surprises and make deployment and operation more repeatable.

## When useful

Use it as a review checklist for networked services and cloud-deployed applications.

## Don't overdo it

It is a model, not a complete architecture. Stateful systems, modern orchestration and regulated environments may require additional or different practices.
