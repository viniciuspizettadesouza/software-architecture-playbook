# Architecture Decision Records

## What they mean

An ADR is a short, versioned record of a significant decision: its context, options, chosen direction and consequences.

## Why they matter

Teams otherwise remember the outcome but lose the constraints and trade-offs that made it reasonable.

## When useful

Record decisions affecting major dependencies, boundaries, public contracts, security, data, deployment or future change cost.

## Don't overdo it

Do not create ADRs for routine implementation details. Supersede old records instead of rewriting history.
