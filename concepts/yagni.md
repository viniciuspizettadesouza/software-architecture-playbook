# YAGNI

## What it means

“You Aren't Gonna Need It”: do not build capabilities solely because they might be useful someday.

## Why it matters

Unused flexibility still has design, test, documentation and maintenance cost. Waiting preserves information until the real requirement is clearer.

## When useful

Use YAGNI when considering generic frameworks, extra deployment modes, premature packages or speculative configuration.

## Don't overdo it

YAGNI is not an excuse to ignore known risks or near-term requirements. Cheap foundations for expensive-to-retrofit qualities can still be justified.
