# KISS

## What it means

Prefer the simplest design that adequately solves the current problem.

## Why it matters

Every additional mechanism creates more behavior to understand, test, operate and change.

## When useful

Use KISS when choosing frameworks, introducing abstractions, splitting services or designing configuration.

## Don't overdo it

Simple is not the same as simplistic. A design that ignores security, reliability or foreseeable scale merely moves complexity into failures.
