# Reproducible Results

## What it means

Results remain traceable to their inputs, environment and execution path through versioned configuration, manifests, hashes and deterministic procedures where practical.

## Why it matters

A result is hard to verify or compare when the data and process that produced it cannot be reconstructed.

## When useful

This is especially important for data science, ML, research, migrations and generated artifacts.

## Trade-offs

Large or protected datasets may require public manifests plus controlled local execution. Some historical results may remain honestly irreproducible.
