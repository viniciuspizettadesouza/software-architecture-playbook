# Project Evidence — Engineering Case Studies

## Context

Executable engineering case studies focused on architecture, trade-offs, accessibility and testing.

## Strong observations

- studies begin as modules rather than separate apps/packages;
- packages are introduced only after a real reuse boundary appears;
- framework-independent domain guidance;
- browser/external capabilities behind adapters when they affect domain/testability;
- deterministic fixtures/simulations;
- explicit rejection of fake infrastructure merely to imitate production;
- monorepo treated as a growth boundary, not a requirement to package everything.
